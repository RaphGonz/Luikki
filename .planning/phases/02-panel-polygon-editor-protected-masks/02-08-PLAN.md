<objective>
Give the panel polygon editor its server: list, create, move-vertex, replace-polygon, delete —
each returning the page's full, freshly reading-ordered panel list.

Purpose: PAN-02 and PAN-03 are "the load-bearing mechanism, not a fallback" (D-19). The detector's
job is to save keystrokes; these five routes are what makes every one of its mistakes one click to
fix. The list-returning shape is the resolved answer to the phase's open question about live
renumbering: the server recomputes, the client renders, and `_reading_order()`'s tiering geometry
is never written twice.

Output: `routers/panel.py`'s routes and their tests.

Implements D-19 (vertex editing plus draw-from-scratch plus delete, with no server-side
confirmation gate on delete) and D-17 (a created panel is still a polygon in page-pixel space).
</objective>

<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| browser → `/api/pages/{page_id}/panels` and `/api/panels/{panel_id}/...` | Artist-supplied polygon geometry and panel ids cross into persistence and, downstream, into rasterisation |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-2-01 | Denial of Service | Polygon bodies on POST/PATCH | mitigate | `Polygon` caps at 512 vertices and `PixelCoord` at `[0, 100000]` (plan 02-05), refused as 422 before the handler runs; tested by `test_polygon_with_too_many_vertices_is_422` |
| T-2-03 | Denial of Service | Geometry outside the page | mitigate | Every create and every vertex/polygon write additionally checks the polygon against `(0, 0, page.width, page.height)` and answers 422 with `POLYGON_OUT_OF_PAGE_DETAIL`; this is the per-page bound the absolute schema bound cannot express |
| T-2-02 | Tampering / Information Disclosure | `panel_id` in the path | mitigate | `_get_owned_panel` resolves the panel then re-checks its page through `get_owned_page`; an id from a closed or foreign project answers 404, tested by `test_unknown_panel_id_is_404` and the `blank_client` case |
| T-2-28 | Denial of Service | A write per `pointermove` during a vertex drag | transfer | The client commits on `pointerup` only (plan 02-10, T-01-FLOOD precedent). Documented in the route docstring so a future client change that floods this route is recognisable as a regression rather than normal traffic |
| T-2-04 | Spoofing | Cross-origin state change | mitigate | Inherited: the router is registered inside `create_app()` under `/api` (plan 02-05), behind `_reject_foreign_origins` |
| T-2-29 | Repudiation | Silent panel loss from a delete with no confirmation | accept | D-19 is explicit that deletion is routine, not destructive-confirmed; UI-SPEC §5 makes in-editor undo the safety net and forbids adding a dialog "to be safe". The Panels gate separately refuses to advance a page with zero panels (plan 02-11) |
</threat_model>

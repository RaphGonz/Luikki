<objective>
Give protected masks the same editing vocabulary panels have — list, draw, move vertex, replace
polygon, delete — plus the one thing panels do not need: a `touched` transition that flips a
detector proposal from "dashed, untouched" to "solid, the artist's".

Purpose: PROT-02 is the guarantee. Panel-boundary and bubble-crossing art is explicitly unsolved
research (the phase goal says so), so the hand-drawn mask is the answer and the detector is the
convenience. Everything here must work identically whether the mask came from `detect_bubbles` or
from the artist's pointer.

Output: `routers/protected.py`'s routes and their tests.

Implements D-20 (page-scoped masks, one row per bubble however many panels it crosses), D-19
(no confirmation on delete), D-24 (kind is chosen by the artist when hand-drawing; the detector
only ever produces bubbles).
</objective>

<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| browser → `/api/pages/{page_id}/protected` and `/api/protected/{mask_id}/...` | Artist-supplied mask geometry and mask ids cross into persistence and into every downstream fill/colour stage's exclusion array |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-2-01 | Denial of Service | Mask polygon bodies | mitigate | `Polygon`'s 512-vertex cap and `PixelCoord`'s range (plan 02-05) refuse oversized geometry with 422 before the handler runs |
| T-2-03 | Denial of Service | Geometry outside the page | mitigate | Every create and write checks the polygon against `(0, 0, page.width, page.height)` and answers 422 with `POLYGON_OUT_OF_PAGE_DETAIL` |
| T-2-07 | Denial of Service | Unbounded mask count per page | mitigate | `MAX_MASKS_PER_PAGE = 256` with a 409 refusal; a page legitimately has tens of bubbles, so a request pushing past 256 is a bug or hostile input, and `protected_bbox_and_area` rasterises once per mask so an unbounded count is unbounded work |
| T-2-02 | Tampering / Information Disclosure | `mask_id` in the path | mitigate | `_get_owned_protected_mask` resolves the mask then re-checks its page through `get_owned_page`; a foreign or stale id answers 404 |
| T-2-04 | Spoofing | Cross-origin state change | mitigate | Inherited: registered inside `create_app()` under `/api` behind `_reject_foreign_origins` |
| T-2-30 | Tampering | A mask silently losing its protection by being stored per-panel | mitigate | D-20 is enforced structurally: the module has no `panel_id` anywhere (asserted by grep) and no per-panel route exists, so a straddling bubble cannot be split into two half-masks that each miss part of the ink |
| T-2-31 | Repudiation | Mask deleted with no confirmation | accept | D-19/UI-SPEC §5: deletion is routine here; in-editor undo is the safety net and a dialog is explicitly forbidden. Unlike panels, the Protected gate is never disabled by mask count — a page with no dialogue is valid |
</threat_model>
